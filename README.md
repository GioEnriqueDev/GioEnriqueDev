### Hi, I'm Giovanni 👋

I'm a **Computer Science student** at **Tor Vergata University of Rome** — and I've shipped **15 B2B platforms into production** before starting that second year.

That order is the whole story: I learned engineering by having users, then went to university to find out what the things I'd built were called.

---

### 🧱 What I actually build

Real systems that real businesses depend on. A few of them:

- **A festival platform** running 15,000 gate entries and hundreds of exhibitors — including a weekly job that works out exactly which compliance document each exhibitor still owes, and chases only that.
- **Cross-continent inventory** (Italy ↔ Miami) — a Next.js back office and a native Expo app on one source of truth, with realtime channels filtered server-side per tenant. There is no refresh button in the product, because there was never a moment one would have helped.
- **A lead-to-contract CRM** — Meta Ads ingestion that survives retries and forged webhooks (timing-safe signature checks, ack-then-defer), and eIDAS e-signature that freezes a real evidence bundle rather than flipping a `signed` flag.

~1,100 commits across those codebases. Nothing teaches idempotency like a webhook that fired twice at 2am.

### 🔒 Why this profile looks quiet

Because the work is client work, and client repos are private. That's the trade of building things people pay for: the commit history isn't public, but the systems are live.

So this profile holds the experiments — a Newton fractal generator, an AI knowledge assistant, an email marketing tool, this portfolio. The real engineering is documented, decision by decision, on my site:
